# 16 · Outputs: siren, strobe and relays

**Status:** In progress - the engine sets its logical outputs (bell, strobe, smoke reset) and
their patterns, host-tested in `components/core/alarm_core`; none of the engine's outputs drives
hardware yet (no `alarm_service` listener takes `on_output`). Left, all BL-037: choosing the
output hardware, the output service with its drivers and temporal timers, then programmable
rules · **Milestone:** M1 (core), later (hardware, rules) ·
**Depends on:** 01, 02, 17

## Goal
Drive the physical siren and strobe from the engine's outputs, and offer programmable
outputs (for example "turn on the porch light during entry delay").

## Reference behaviour
A keypad alarm panel has a bell output (steady for burglary, temporal for fire, timed out by the
bell timeout) and programmable outputs (relays or powerline devices) triggered by events such as
alarm, arm/disarm, zone fault or trouble, with actions like close, open, pulse or follow.

## Engine outputs (implemented)

| Output | Scope | Patterns | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|
| `BELL` | partition | `steady` (burglary, audible panic, tamper, 24 h audible), `temporal3` (fire), `temporal4` (CO), `test` (1 s pulse at walk-test start), `off` | Burglary times out after the bell timeout; fire/CO sound until silenced (default) |
| `STROBE` | partition | `on` / `off` | On with any audible alarm, stays on until disarm |
| `SMOKE_RESET` | system | `on` / `off` | Interrupts 4-wire smoke detector power: fire memory clear, fire verification |

Keypads mirror the alarm on their own speakers ([08](../08-Audio-and-Annunciation/README.md)).

## Design
- `outputs` service on the master: receives `on_output` from the alarm task, maps logical
  outputs to physical channels (configuration), and runs pattern timers (temporal-3/4) in a
  high-priority timer so they stay exact.
- Programmable rules (later): the panel's one rule table is docs/features/41's automations
  (ADR-049); a relay becomes an automation action (on, off) once outputs exist, with the zone
  lists in a field 41's stored format already reserves. Pulse and follow stay output functions.
- Hardware is your side: a 12 V siren needs its own supply and a driver (relay or logic-level
  MOSFET) and must never be powered from a GPIO.

## Open questions
