How it works
  1. 1This pageYou pick the words. Only their clip numbers and the pause between words go to the panel: no audio.
  2. 2PanelIt takes each word from its own voice pack and joins them with that pause, as it does for a zone's name.
  3. 3PanelIts Voice volume sets the loudness, 3 dB for every 10 %: at 70 % (the default) the pack plays as it is, below that quieter, above it louder - up to +9 dB of gain at 100 %, heard as about +4.7 dB because the words already peak near the top and its limiter takes the rest back. Then its speaker plays it.
